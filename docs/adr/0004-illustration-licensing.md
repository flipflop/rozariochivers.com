# 0004. Illustrations: original SVG plus CC0/public-domain art; the 1958 book is reference only

- **Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-02

## Context
The look depends on period illustration. The 1958 almanac's copyright status could not be verified (HathiTrust blocked automated checks; American Aviation Publications holds the notice). Popular "free" sets (unDraw, Humaaans) are flat modern styles or carry custom licences.

## Decision
Use original SVG drawn for the site and CC0/public-domain figures from Openclipart and Wikimedia Commons, recoloured and composed into the house style, each credited in `assets/illustrations/CREDITS.md`. Owner-supplied images (portrait, TV face, screenshots) are used with permission. Reproduce nothing from the 1958 book. Character design (the lounge robot) is an homage to the era, not a replica of a trademarked character.

## Consequences
No licensing risk on a public sales site. Some figures are 1910s–40s rather than strictly 1950s; mitigated by consistent recolouring and composition.
