# 0001. Present the portfolio as a 1958 almanac

- **Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-02 · supersedes the earlier tDR-style site in `../site/`

## Context
A CTO portfolio must read as considered and distinctive without slipping into generic "dark mode tech" or self-promotion. Roz supplied *Aviation Facts and Figures, 1958* as the visual reference.

## Decision
Build the site as a companion volume, *Technology Facts and Figures, 2026*: two-ink print aesthetic, numbered project chapters, almanac charts, a route-map career and a departures-board index. The earlier site is kept only as a content reference.

## Consequences
Every design choice has a test ("could a 1958 printer do this?"). The metaphor constrains palette and type (see DESIGN.md) and gives natural homes for content (chapters, flight log, command). It also sets a high bar for illustration quality.
