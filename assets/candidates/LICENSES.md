## Not downloaded / notes
- Open Peeps, Open Doodles, Humaaans: CC0 but flat or doodle styles and not 1950s realism; not fetched. unDraw: custom licence (free commercial use, no attribution, but no redistribution as an asset set), not CC0; not fetched.
- Wikimedia Commons, openclipart: no genuine 1950s realistic walking-businessman, gardener or sword-wielding explorer found in PD SVG form. The Archive.org, NYPL, Flickr Commons and rawpixel collections were not reached (no API access in this session) and are untested.
- Excluded (CC BY-SA, e.g. the "Cartoon Man ... Computer" series on Commons) and removed (poor style fit: typing-in-bed, secretary-dictating).
- Ad-derived Commons files are old US adverts tagged PD by Commons contributors; logos/brand text (e.g. Monarch) may carry trademark considerations, though not copyright.
